# 📖 About Socio — The Story & Vision

> **"ChatGPT is a tool you open. Socio is a partner that lives in your pocket."**

Socio was born out of a simple, crucial realization during a 24-hour hackathon: **solo founders do not fail because they lack intelligence or work ethic. They fail because they make critical decisions alone.** 

---

## 🎯 The Thinking Partner Insight

While every startup tool in the market (Notion, Linear, Slack, ChatGPT) focuses on solving **productivity**, nobody is addressing the **isolation** of solo founding. Productivity tools keep you organized, but they don't challenge your assumptions, cheer you on during late nights, or outline long-term unit economics when you're overwhelmed. 

Socio is not a tool. It is a **thinking partner**. This core philosophy drives every design, feature, and code decision in the system.

---

## ⚡ The Origin Story

Socio was created by **Deepak Pandey** (Team **Doppelganger**) during the **QuantCraft 2026 Hackathon** at Galgotias University. Out of 250 initial teams, the project stood out and was selected for the prestigious offline 24-hour final round (top 50). 

During ideation, the team filtered through many standard hackathon templates (including AI mock interviewers, plant disease detectors, and RFP builders) but discarded them to focus on a solution that combined B2C scalability, emotional design, and high pitch demonstration value. 

### Why the name "Doppelganger"?
We chose our team name because that is exactly what a true co-founder is: **your thinking double**, standing alongside you, anticipating your moves, challenging your blind spots, and keeping you aligned with the goal.

---

## 🎭 The Adaptive Persona Architecture

Most AI products force founders to adjust their mental state to the machine—selecting clinical parameters, toggling drop-downs, or typing detailed structural rules in a prompt. 

Socio's **Adaptive Persona Engine** reverses this dynamic:
1.  **Zero Overhead**: You just type or speak. No manual mode toggling.
2.  **Instant Sentiment Classification**: A fast, low-cost classifier analyzes your message's emotional tone, urgency, and intent.
3.  **Dynamic Weight Allocation**: The system calculates a custom blend of weights for our three core advisor styles: **The Skeptic**, **The Hustler**, and **The Strategist**.
4.  **Master Blending**: The system prompt processes these active weights, enabling Gemini to adjust its vocabulary, intensity, and strategic focus instantly.

## 🎨 Warm Minimalist Design System

Drawing inspiration from **Pi AI** (the first truly conversational emotional AI system), Socio features a premium, non-clinical aesthetic designed to evoke a human connection:
*   **Warm Tones**: Utilizes warm cream off-white background foundations instead of clinical hospital-blue dark modes.
*   **Deep Purples & Forest Greens**: Active highlights convey high professionalism and a premium finish.
*   **Peers-to-Peer Typography**: Styled using clean, premium Google Fonts (Inter, Outfit, and DM Sans) to look elegant and human.

*   Up to 20 AI conversational text turns per day.
