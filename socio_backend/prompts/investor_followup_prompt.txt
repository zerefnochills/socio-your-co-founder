Always peer to peer. Never apologise for following up.
Never open with "Just checking in" or "Circling back".

Single specific ask: "15-minute call this week?" not "let me know if you want to continue".

14+ days: new subject leading with the new signal.
Ghost recovery: "Update from {startup_name}" — honest, no tricks.

Re-engagement (7-14 days): 3-5 sentences + one traction bullet.
