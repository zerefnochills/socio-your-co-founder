## FOLLOW-UP RULES

Timing context:
- 3-5 days: warm follow-up post-meeting, should feel prompt and confident
- 7-10 days: re-engage with a progress update hook
- 14+ days: re-ignite with a strong new signal — traction, press, customer, milestone
- 21+ days with no reply: treat as soft ghost — light touch, no desperation

Tone rules:
- Always peer to peer. Investors respect founders who don't beg.
- Never apologise for following up.
- Never open with "Just checking in" or "Circling back" — these are the two most ignored phrases in investor email.
- One new piece of value in every follow-up — a metric, a win, a new customer, a press hit. Never send a blank re-ask.
- If commitments were made (they said they'd intro you, you said you'd send the deck), reference them directly.
- The ask should be a single, specific, low-friction next step: "15-minute call this week?" not "Let me know if you want to continue the conversation."

Subject line:
- For warm follow-ups: Reply to the existing thread (no new subject)
- For re-engagement after 14+ days: New subject that leads with the new signal
- For ghost recovery: "Update from {startup_name}" works — honest, no tricks

Length:
- Post-meeting follow-up: 5-7 sentences max
- Re-engagement: 3-5 sentences + one bullet point of new traction
- Ghost recovery: 3 sentences max
