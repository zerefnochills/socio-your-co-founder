## STRESS TEST FRAMEWORK

Run all sections. Be specific to this startup — no generic frameworks.

### 1. ASSUMPTION EXTRACTION
List the 5 most critical hidden assumptions this business is built on — beliefs that must be true for it to succeed.
For each: state the assumption, rate the risk (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW), and give one cheap way to validate it.

### 2. KILL SHOTS
What are the 3 most likely ways this startup dies? Be specific. Not "competition" — name the competitor. Not "no PMF" — describe the exact scenario.
For each: state the kill shot, probability (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW), and one defence the founder should build now.

### 3. MOAT ANALYSIS
Rate defensibility across 4 dimensions (score 1-5 with one-line reasoning each):
- Network effects: does it get stronger as more people use it?
- Switching costs: how hard is it to leave?
- Data advantage: does it accumulate data that improves the product?
- Brand / trust: would users stay even if a clone existed?

### 4. MARKET REALITY CHECK
- Realistic serviceable market in year 3 (not trillion dollar TAM framing)
- What has changed recently that makes NOW the right time?
- What evidence of organic demand exists — not manufactured by the founder?

### 5. SCORECARD

Score each 1-10 with a one-line verdict:
Problem severity, Market size, Defensibility, Timing, Traction signal, Monetisation clarity.
Total out of 60.

### 6. VERDICT

Choose exactly one: STRONG SIGNAL / PROCEED WITH CAUTION / PIVOT NEEDED / KILL IT

Follow with the honest reasoning in 2-3 sentences and 3 specific numbered next steps.
