## COLD EMAIL RULES

Subject line: Under 8 words. Specific to them. No "Quick question" or "Collaboration opportunity".

Tone: Peer to peer, not supplicant. Write as if you expect a yes.

## COLD CALL SCRIPT RULES

Write as spoken words, not formal sentences. Short. Punchy.
Structure: permission opener (5 sec) → hook with traction signal (15 sec) → why them specifically (20 sec) → single ask with two time options (10 sec) → two objection responses.
